OSINT geolocation guide

Move from broad location clues to defensible geolocation and chronolocation using independent visual, geographic and temporal evidence.

Read the scene

Road geometry, signs, architecture, terrain, vegetation, utilities and transport systems.

Compare maps

Use OpenStreetMap, public satellite imagery and street-level material where available.

Test time claims

Weather, sun angle, shadows, seasonality and publication history can support chronolocation.

Build a confidence case

Prefer multiple independent features and record what remains unresolved.

Practice with purpose-built or safely public material. Do not turn geolocation exercises into stalking or sensitive-location targeting.
