Learn from established investigation resources

Use tool directories for discovery, then learn the method from resources such as Bellingcat’s Online Investigations Toolkit, OSINT Dojo, GIJN’s Resource Center and the Verification Handbook. The full library links to these learning resources.

Privacy and authorization: keep collection proportional to the research question. This roadmap focuses on lawful public-source research and passive enrichment, not private access, deceptive collection or unauthorized active probing.
