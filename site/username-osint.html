Workflow

Discover

Check the exact handle across public services and search engines. Record only relevant public matches.

Compare stable signals

Look for consistent profile links, long-lived aliases, public project references, dates, language or other independent context.

Challenge the match

Ask whether the same handle could plausibly belong to different people. Common or recycled usernames require stronger corroboration.

Report carefully

Separate a handle match from a person-level attribution. State confidence and the evidence that supports it.

Privacy rule: minimize collection, avoid private or sensitive data, and do not turn public-source research into stalking, harassment or doxxing.
