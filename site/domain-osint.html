Passive workflow

Registration context

Start with ICANN/RDAP and official registry data. Record the observation date because privacy services and registrant details change.

Certificates and names

Certificate Transparency can reveal historical hostnames and certificate relationships. Treat them as observations tied to issuance time.

DNS and routing

Use passive DNS, RIPEstat/BGP context and public service indexes to understand infrastructure without actively probing systems.

Historical content

Web archives can show how a domain was used at different times. Keep ownership changes and recycled domains in mind.

Attribution warning: shared hosting, CDNs, registrars, certificates and IP reuse can create relationships that are operational rather than ownership evidence.
