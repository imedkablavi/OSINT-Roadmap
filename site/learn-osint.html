OSINT Learning Path

How to learn OSINT

A practical sequence for beginners who want to become reliable open-source investigators rather than tool collectors.

HomeTools

1. Foundations

Define the question, legal scope, privacy boundaries and evidence standard.

2. Discovery

Search engines, archives, public records, multilingual queries, usernames and domains.

3. Verification

Trace provenance, compare independent sources, validate timelines, images and claims.

4. Analysis

Test alternative hypotheses, separate correlation from attribution and assess confidence.

5. Reporting

Record scope, findings, evidence, method, limitations and confidence rationale.

6. Specialize

Choose CTI, GEOINT, digital footprint, company research or investigative journalism workflows.

Use the Skill Matrix, practice labs and playbooks to turn each stage into work you can defend.
