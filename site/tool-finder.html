Good defaults by investigation type

I have a domain

Start with ICANN Lookup, crt.sh, RIPEstat, urlscan.io and historical web archives. Add Shodan/Censys only when the research question needs already-observed service context.

I have an image

Use Google Lens, TinEye and Yandex as complementary indexes. Then inspect metadata, landmarks, chronology and original publication context.

I have a company name

Resolve the legal entity with OpenCorporates or an official registry, then use LEI, filings, sanctions and investigative datasets as additional layers.

I have a location claim

Cross-check maps, street-level imagery, terrain, sun direction and satellite imagery. One visual clue is rarely enough.

I have an IP or IOC

Prefer passive enrichment: VirusTotal, urlscan.io, Censys, Shodan, GreyNoise, RIPEstat and abuse.ch datasets. Keep scan time and data age in mind.

I have a username

Use username tools for discovery, then manually compare independent signals. Handle reuse alone is weak attribution.

Learn the method behind the tools

Tools change faster than good investigation practice. Use the research methods, investigation playbooks and report template with this finder.
