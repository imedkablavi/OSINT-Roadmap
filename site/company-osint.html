Entity-first workflow

Resolve the entity

Capture the legal name, jurisdiction, registration number and official registry source before collecting secondary references.

Read primary filings

Use official company registries, securities filings and LEI records to establish dates, directors, reported parents and legal status.

Layer external datasets

Sanctions, offshore and investigative datasets can add leads. Preserve dataset provenance and verify high-impact claims against primary records where possible.

Build a timeline

Ownership, addresses and directors change. Time-bound every relationship instead of treating the latest record as historical truth.

Entity-resolution rule: matching names do not prove matching entities. Require jurisdiction, identifiers, addresses, filings or other corroborating data.
